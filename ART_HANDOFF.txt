CaptureMyMonth3D — next art pass

ART DIRECTION
A hand-crafted miniature valley. Soft rounded rocks, warm illuminated paths,
cool water, layered vegetation, gentle snow, small cottages, and a tactile traveler.
Maintain the tall island silhouette and the original three-quarter overview.
Winter is the first theme pack, not the product identity.

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION
Environment primitives and the character rig are authored in TypeScript/Three.js.
The browser renders actual geometry. No external paid artwork is required.
The scene's route and the calendar engine are independent of individual prop meshes.

BLENDER DELIVERABLES TO ADD
1. valley.glb — modular terrain chunks and static landmarks.
2. traveler.glb — one rig with idle, walk, run, read, write, type, stretch, wave, celebrate clips.
3. study.glb, cafe.glb, campfire.glb, travel.glb — small action-stage prop sets.
4. winter-theme.json — palette, light settings, decorations, and environment choice.
5. three card miniatures — travel, achievement, and connections.

EXPORT CONTRACT
Use glTF/GLB, Y-up, meters. Apply transforms. Place the character's origin at its feet.
Keep the walk/run clips in place; the journey engine owns route movement.
Name interaction sockets consistently: activity_origin, character_anchor,
camera_target, prop_table, companion_left, companion_right.
Keep static lightmap/AO assets distinct from dynamic character lighting.
Blender-only procedural shader graphs must be baked or recreated for the web.
Keep scene generations versioned so stored journey positions remain reproducible.

TEN-SECOND SHOT
0.0–2.1: full diorama; warm path establishes the route.
2.1–5.0: ease into the traveler at the first milestone.
5.0–6.8: walk a few steps to the first activity.
6.8–10.0: study/write gesture; sidebar highlights Nov 1, Weekly Study Review.
The full journey continues from the same clock after the preview.

QUALITY GATES
- No foot sliding or floating on the path.
- Rounded silhouettes remain appealing at both overview and interaction scale.
- The traveler, activity prop, and current milestone can be read in one glance.
- Lights glow without washing out the entire road.
- Separate performance measurements for desktop and target mobile devices.
- Preserve an overview framing where no part of the island is cut off.
